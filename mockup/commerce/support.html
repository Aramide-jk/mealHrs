Common questions

Who delivers my order?

The food vendor arranges and fulfills delivery. MealHrs provides ordering, payment status and support.

How do I track a refund?

Your order page shows the verified refund status. A pending refund is never shown as completed.

Can I change an address after ordering?

Contact support immediately. Changes are not guaranteed after a vendor accepts the order.
